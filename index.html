First-time setup with your own Google Sheet
  1. Create a Google Sheet, then open Extensions › Apps Script.
  2. Tap Copy backend script above. Paste it over everything in the editor and save. A private token is generated for you and already filled in.
  3. In the toolbar choose the function setup and click Run. Approve the permissions (Sheets and Calendar).
  4. Deploy › New deployment › Web app. Execute as Me, access Anyone. Copy the Web app URL.
  5. Paste the URL above and tap Save connection.
  6. On your other devices, enter the same URL and the token from the SECRET line in Apps Script.

Plans & calendar

Waiting for the first sync
Auto-log plans when they endEach planned activity is logged for you once it ends, so you can plan and log from Google Calendar alone. Delete or move it before it ends if plans change, or tap “Didn’t happen”. Plans that ended before you turned this on stay in To confirm.
Update your backend script (needed for plans & calendars)
  1. Tap Copy backend script above. In your Google Sheet open Extensions › Apps Script, select everything in the editor and paste over it. Save.
  2. Check the SECRET line near the top holds your token (the button fills it in for you).
  3. In the toolbar choose the function setup and click Run. Approve the permissions; Google asks for Calendar access and to run a background task.
  4. Deploy › Manage deployments › pencil icon › Version: New version › Deploy. Your Web app URL stays the same.
  5. Come back here and tap Sync now.
See it in the iPhone Calendar app
  1. On your iPhone open Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Add Account › Google and sign in with the Google account that owns your sheet.
  2. Make sure Calendars is switched on for that account.
  3. In the Calendar app tap Calendars and tick Timesheet Plans and Timesheet Log.

Timesheet Plans holds what’s still ahead. Add an event there from your iPhone or Google Calendar and it shows up here as a plan to confirm. Timesheet Log mirrors everything you log that has a start and end time; when you confirm a plan it moves from Plans to Log at the actual time. Hide either calendar any time with its checkbox.

Deleting works both ways for plans: delete a plan in the app or in Timesheet Plans and it disappears from the other. Deleting one week of a repeating plan in the calendar counts as “Didn’t happen” for that week. To change the time of a repeating plan, edit it in the app. Logged entries are deleted in the app only, so a stray tap in your calendar can never erase your hours.

With Auto-log on you can work from your calendar alone: add an event to Timesheet Plans (put the tag code in the title, like “CM with Isaac”), delete or drag it if plans change, and once it ends it’s logged automatically and moves to Timesheet Log with a ✓.
